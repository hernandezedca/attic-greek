One dialect, deliberately

Attic Greek, specifically.

This is a course in Attic Greek — the dialect of fifth- and fourth-century Athens: Plato, Aristotle, Thucydides, the tragedians. Every lesson, flashcard and quiz is built on it. Among the 45 authentic readings you will also meet a few passages of Homer and the New Testament, annotated with notes on how their Greek differs from Attic — context for the language, not a second curriculum.

Getting started

Do I need prior knowledge of Greek to use the app? ▾
No prior knowledge is needed — or assumed. The course begins with the Greek alphabet and the sounds of each letter before moving into grammar. If you have never seen a Greek letter, this is exactly where to start.
Which dialect of Ancient Greek does the app teach? ▾
The primary focus is Attic Greek — the dialect of Plato, Aristotle, Thucydides and the Athenian dramatists. The 45 authentic readings also include passages in Homeric Greek (Homer) and Koine Greek (the New Testament), annotated with notes on how each differs from Attic.
What do I get for free? ▾
Lessons 1 to 5 — the Greek alphabet, breathings and accents, the definite article, and the first and second declensions — together with their quizzes and vocabulary, plus one complete annotated reading: the opening of Homer’s Odyssey (Ἄνδρα μοι ἔννεπε, Μοῦσα), with translation, grammatical notes and vocabulary. All of it is free permanently: no account, no card, no countdown. A subscription unlocks the remaining 205 lessons, all 900 flashcards and the other 44 readings.

Learning & Content

How are the 210 lessons structured? ▾
The curriculum progresses from the Greek alphabet through elementary morphology (nouns, verbs, adjectives, pronouns) into intermediate syntax (participles, infinitives, subjunctive and optative moods) and finally advanced constructions (indirect statement, conditions, purpose clauses) — the grammar necessary to read authentic Homer and Plato without assistance.
What texts are included in the 45 authentic readings? ▾
Readings include fifteen passages from Homer's Odyssey (plus the Iliad), Plato (Apology, Republic, Symposium, Phaedo, Phaedrus, Meno), Aristotle (Nicomachean Ethics, Politics, Metaphysics, Physics), Thucydides, Heraclitus, Parmenides, Marcus Aurelius, and the famous inscription at Delphi. All readings are annotated with vocabulary aids and grammatical commentary.
Is this app suitable for university Classics students? ▾
Yes. The grammar curriculum follows a rigorous scholarly sequence comparable to standard university Classics textbooks. Many students use the app to supplement university coursework, review grammar between seminars, or maintain progress during holidays.
I'm studying Koine Greek for seminary / biblical studies. Is this useful? ▾
Attic and Koine Greek share the same foundational grammar, vocabulary, and script. A strong Attic foundation makes Koine significantly easier. The app includes Koine readings from the New Testament annotated with notes on how Koine differs from Attic. Many theology and seminary students have found the app valuable as a first step before specialised Koine resources.
